## Design Specificity Verdict

Split verdict. The mechanism screens (ScanBarcodeScreen, ForgottenProductsSheet, SmartShoppingListScreen, the async ticket pipeline) are genuinely authored for the confirmed "passive assistant, ticket-history-first" positioning. The shell still reads as a generic comparador+rewards template: AuthWelcomeScreen sells price-comparison (the explicit contrast case in PRODUCT.md), BottomNav gives Points a permanent tab and ticket history none, onboarding teaches offers before history, and the app's most on-positioning gesture (tapping a product from a just-processed ticket) terminates in ComparePricesScreen, the least on-positioning screen.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs still gives no signal (exit 0, empty) — confirmed no RN rules. Manual evidence: 314 raw hex literals (92 exact duplicates of existing tokens), 462 raw fontSize literals vs 18 token references, new radii/space tokens at 0 of 1,170 possible usages, 25 screens hand-roll an identical header block with no shared component, useThemeColors used in only 2 of 56 files, 14 backend-fed lists across 9 screens still using ScrollView+map instead of FlatList, and Pressable-to-accessibility-prop coverage at 32-34%.

Visual overlays: not applicable — native app, no web build.

## Overall Impression

Real, verified progress since the last pass (QR confirmed dead, referral system built and wired, real backend replacing mock for offers/products/stores/maps) sits next to new fabrication that is arguably worse than what was fixed: PaymentMethodsScreen solicits a real card number under a false encryption claim, and the referral/rewards economy fabricates a subscription business complete with a regenerated fake redemption code and a real cross-account privacy leak (points/history survive logout and bleed into the next account). The single biggest opportunity: "no fabricar lo que no existe" was applied once as a point fix (the QR), not as a sweep — at least four more live instances of the same pattern exist today.

## What's Working

1. ScanBarcodeScreen — a model of data honesty: cites SEPA source and date, shows branch counts, distinguishes two kinds of "we don't know" via fuenteDatos instead of a generic empty state.
2. The async ticket pipeline — the positioning made real: non-blocking upload, awaitingTicketIds deliberately lifted to App.tsx to survive navigation, ForgottenProductsSheet never repeats. Every decision here subtracts user work.
3. The offer-card information design — amountTile isolates the number, the HASTA kicker marks a ceiling not a rate, appliesChipWarm is reserved specifically for conditional mechanics. Real craft that holds up.

## Priority Issues

[P0] PaymentMethodsScreen fabricates saved cards, solicits a real card number, and asserts a false security guarantee
- Why it matters: the most severe live breach of "no fabricar lo que no existe" — worse than the QR, because it fakes a security property while actively soliciting payment credentials, for a product with nothing to charge for.
- Fix: delete the screen (and its ProfileScreen/App.tsx wiring), or at minimum remove INITIAL_CARDS, remove the card-number field, and replace the encryption claim with the truth in both this screen and HelpCenterScreen FAQ #3.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P0] The referral/rewards economy fabricates a subscription business, the redeem code repeats the fake-QR pattern, and balances leak across accounts
- Why it matters: rewards.ts states billing operations as present fact; generateCode() produces a Math.random() code that lives in no system; RegisterStep1 grants 20 points for any non-empty referral string; and handleLogout never clears referralPoints/referralHistory, so one account's balance and history carry into the next login on the device — a real privacy bug, not just an inconsistency.
- Fix: reframe rewards.ts copy in the conditional tense, remove the fake redeem code in favor of "we'll notify you," and clear referralPoints/referralHistory on logout at minimum.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] ComparePricesScreen and StoreDetailScreen are now the app's only fiction and stand out sharply; StoreDetail invents actionable facts about real businesses
- Why it matters: with everything else now real, these two screens are the only place the app lies — and StoreDetailScreen invents a phone number, hours, and "open now" status for named real chains, next to a hand-drawn fake map 100 lines from the real MapView already in FavoriteStoresScreen.
- Fix: migrate StoreDetailScreen to the real MapView and storesApi data first (cheap, high-harm); migrate ComparePricesScreen to getProductoPorEan's comercios[] next.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] The confirmed core mechanism (ticket history) has no permanent nav tab
- Why it matters: IA is the product's most honest self-statement. Points (frontend-only) owns a tab; ticket history (the confirmed core mechanism) owns none.
- Fix: replace the Points tab with a Tickets/history tab; move referrals into a ProfileScreen row; reorder onboarding to scan → history → offers.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

[P1] A "Cancelar" button permanently deletes the user's ticket with no confirmation
- Why it matters: "Cancelar" universally means abandon the action, not destroy the object — here it destroys the app's highest-value asset server-side.
- Fix: relabel to a destructive "Descartar ticket," gate behind a confirm dialog.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

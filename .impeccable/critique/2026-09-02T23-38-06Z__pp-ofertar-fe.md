## Design Specificity Verdict

Argentine on the surface, generic rewards-app underneath (~30% authored, 70% reskinnable). Native voseo, real store brands/colors, and ScanBarcodeScreen's SEPA-backed data are genuinely specific. But the rewards catalogue is 50% Starbucks/Rappi, inflation (the defining fact of Argentine grocery shopping) is absent from the design, and PaymentMethodsScreen simulates stored cards in an app that charges nothing.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs ran clean (exit 0) but returned zero findings — not built for RN StyleSheet patterns, no signal either way. Manual grep evidence: 405 raw fontSize literals vs 18 typography.sizes references (concentrated in one file); 197 raw hex literals (57 distinct) outside the theme file, 20 of which exactly duplicate an existing token; 20 of 49 screens (41%) copy-paste an identical header style block with no shared ScreenHeader component.

Visual overlays: not applicable — native app, no web build, no viewable URL.

## Overall Impression

Strong visual idea (navy hero cards) and one screen that proves the team understands its real user (ScanBarcodeScreen, SEPA-backed). But the screen most users actually reach for price comparison (ComparePricesScreen) shows hardcoded, fake prices. The biggest missed opportunity is product-level: 8 screens and a full nav tab go to a rewards system whose two worst trust failures (fake QR, phantom points balance) live there, while the one truly differentiated capability (real SEPA price data) is a footnote.

## What's Working

1. LoadingOverlay — staged messages and a breathing pulse animation turn OCR wait time into anticipation; the only place the emotional register was deliberately tuned.
2. ScanBarcodeScreen — cites its data source and freshness (SEPA dataset date, sucursal counts), distinguishes two kinds of "no data," and has a real empty state. Showing your homework is the product.
3. The navy hero-card pattern — repeats across Home/Points/MonthlyAnalysis/TicketProcessed with one strong idea (overline + big figure + cyan supporting metrics); the one piece of visual language that survives consistently.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Fake, unscannable QR code presented at the moment of public offer redemption
- Why it matters: OfferCodeScreen synthesizes a non-scannable QR pattern from a seeded PRNG with no indication it's a placeholder. Users discover this in front of a cashier, mid-queue — the single highest-consequence design decision in the codebase.
- Fix: Remove the fake QR; show a large, copyable alphanumeric code with explicit instructions, or integrate a real QR library encoding the actual code.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] Points balance is a hardcoded constant that never decrements
- Why it matters: SALDO_PUNTOS never updates after redemption — the same reward can be "redeemed" infinitely and the balance is permanently wrong. USAS and QUEDA also use two different number formats side by side on the same confirmation screen.
- Fix: Persist and decrement the balance on confirm; unify number formatting with toLocaleString("es-AR").
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] The design system exists as a file, not a practice
- Why it matters: 405 raw fontSize literals vs 18 token references (one file); 197 raw hex literals vs 17 color tokens (20 exact duplicates); 41% of screens copy-paste an un-componentized header. This is why the app reads as assembled rather than designed, and why the new dark-mode tokens can't roll out cleanly.
- Fix: Extend typography.sizes to sizes actually in use, add radii/space tokens, add semantic color tokens, extract a shared ScreenHeader component.
- Suggested command: /impeccable extract

[P1] Dead controls cluster exactly where users are already stuck
- Why it matters: Help search is a View not a TextInput; "Contactar soporte" has no onPress in two different screens; the 4 compare-price filter chips change state and touch nothing; the offers-tab notification bell shows a fake unread dot. Each one turns a moment of "I need help" into "this app is broken."
- Fix: Ship-or-hide each control — wire support to mailto/WhatsApp, make the FAQ search real, make filters actually re-sort, remove the bell until notifications exist.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

[P2] Home fabricates purchase history for zero-ticket users; almost no empty/error states exist
- Why it matters: TRACKED_PRODUCTS renders unconditionally under "productos que comprás seguido" even at $0/0 tickets — the first screen after onboarding shows a provably false claim. 2 of 5 offer categories have zero offers and no ListEmptyComponent.
- Fix: Branch Home into a real first-run state below a ticket-count threshold; give getSavingsReport an error/retry branch; add ListEmptyComponent to OffersScreen.
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): Registration progress bar claims "step 1/2 of 3" but there is no step 3 — the real flow is 4 more screens plus a 4-step tour. Day-one Home shows fabricated "products you buy often." Two onboarding systems exist in the code; the better one (OnboardingScreen, guided tour) is never mounted in App.tsx.

Casey (distracted mobile): Home offer cards nest an "Activar oferta" button inside the whole-card tap target — a one-handed mistap irreversibly activates an offer. TicketProcessedScreen routes a tap on any product row away from the unsaved-verification screen instead of into edit. Critical labels render at 9px with ~1.7:1 contrast.

Riley (stress tester): Redeeming the same reward three times leaves the balance unchanged everywhere. The month-forward chevron on MonthlyAnalysisScreen no-ops silently at the current month. Android back from RedeemSuccessScreen returns to a completed ConfirmRedeemScreen with "Confirmar canje" still live.

## Minor Observations

- ProfileScreen hardcodes "Nivel Plata · 2.430 pts" as a string while PointsScreen derives it from SALDO_PUNTOS — two sources for one number.
- ScanTicketScreen.tsx is exported and never rendered — an orphaned third scan screen.
- ComparePricesScreen mixes an emoji into a data field (rank: "Mas barato" with a medal emoji) next to plain-text ordinals.
- RedeemSuccessScreen hardcodes a fixed 2026 expiry date that contradicts the reward.validity field.
- Auth-flow primary buttons use 13px labels while the rest of the app uses 15px for primary CTAs.

## Questions to Consider

- If the SEPA dataset is public, why does the screen most users actually reach (ComparePricesScreen) still show invented prices instead of the real ones ScanBarcodeScreen already pulls?
- Is the scanned receipt the input, or is it the product? It's the most valuable personal-inflation dataset a shopper could own, and MonthlyAnalysisScreen never separates the user's own spending increase from the country's inflation.
- What is the points economy for, and who is it serving? 8 screens and a full nav tab carry the app's two worst trust failures (fake QR, phantom balance) for its least differentiated feature.
- If Home were rebuilt around "what should I buy this week, and where," would the retailer offers carousel survive the cut?
